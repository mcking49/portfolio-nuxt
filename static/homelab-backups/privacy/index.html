Privacy Policy

Homelab Backups is a private tool used only by its owner.

Access: The app requests Google's drive.file scope to create and manage its backup files. It does not access unrelated Drive content.

Use and storage: Backup data is encrypted locally before upload to Google Drive. Credentials are protected using 1Password. Google Drive data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for analytics.

Retention and control: Backups remain in Drive until deleted by the owner or the configured retention process. Access can be revoked in Google Account settings; revocation does not delete existing backup files.
